Big organisations have strategy teams to help them think. Small founders have themselves.

I’ve lived on both sides of that gap. I learned my craft inside organisations, from quality systems on factory floors in Chennai to strategy rooms in the UAE, where I saw that most problems aren’t caused by people, but by how the work is set up around them.

Then I built a business of my own. My brother and I started a sportswear brand from nothing and ran it for five years, with late-night calls and every risk shared between two people. That taught me what no framework had: how heavy decisions feel when they’re all yours.

Everything I build now starts there.

How I work

Fix the system, not the people

When work keeps breaking, the cause is almost always how it is set up. Change the set-up and good people do great work.

Give judgement a better room

Data and AI earn their place when they help people decide, not when they decide for them. Technology should make the picture clearer and the choice yours.

Build for the long run

Patient, quiet work compounds. I’d rather build something that is still standing in ten years than something that is loud this year.
